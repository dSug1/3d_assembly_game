Glass Four

A glass 90° V6 on a wooden plinth. The two banks of three cylinders lean out from a glass crankcase. A single bent chrome crankshaft with three throws carries one rod from each bank on every throw. Each cylinder has its two valves set across the bank, ending in hammered caps. Chrome exhausts run along each side: one short stub per cylinder leaves the outer face of its bank at a right angle, feeding a collector whose rear end is open. A hammered pulley faces front. Nothing sits in the V, so you look straight down between the banks.

How it moves

  1. Each crank throw drives one piston in each bank, 90° apart because of the bank angle.
  2. For every piston, the first valve opens at the top and closes at the bottom. During the next full up-and-down stroke neither valve moves. The second valve then opens at the bottom and closes at the top.
  3. The six cylinders start their cycles at evenly spread points over two crank turns, so the caps ripple across both banks. The loop is 720°.

Rig

One Empty, crank, with X rotation keyed 0→720° and linear interpolation.

Pistons: parent each bank to an Empty rotated ±45° about X. In bank space the piston travel is y + sqrt(l*l - z*z), where (y, z) is the crank pin seen from that bank.

Crankshaft: draw it as one curve (straight runs with right-angle jogs out to the throws), add a round bevel and fillet the corners. It is a single object.

Rods: Child Of the crank pin, plus a Damped Track to the wrist pin.

Valves: with p = (θ − TDC) mod 720 for each cylinder, the first valve lifts by sin(π·p/180) for p from 0 to 180, and the second by sin(π·(p−540)/180) for p from 540 to 720. Otherwise both stay shut, with exhaust from 160 to 380 and intake from 340 to 560. Firing angles are 0, 540, 180, 360 for cylinders 1 to 4.

Look

Wood: a medium-light beech or cherry with long vertical grain, satin finish, roughness 0.45.

Chrome: metallic 1, roughness 0.15, with a coarse Voronoi into Bump for the hammered caps and smooth everywhere else.

Glass: a Glass BSDF with IOR 1.45 and roughness 0. Bevel every edge so the tubes catch rim light. Render in Cycles; a white seamless backdrop with blind-stripe light completes the reference look.
