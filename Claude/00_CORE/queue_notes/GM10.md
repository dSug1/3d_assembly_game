A TAP on a part whose step is available (outlined) picks it; tap again on the other candidate as the immediate next input cycles; a DOUBLE TAP on the
held piece puts it down on the free spot nearest it by the boot's rules, the least turn to rest; a single tap on the held piece waits for the
double-tap window; a pick and a put-down cost one episode each. No animation yet: the poses are spawned. After `CL1`.
