## The owner's follow-ups (screenshots)

1. **"A small dy pushes the piece from one position to the other with a 180° roll of the camera"** — measured headless: over the top, the screen's
   up turned **177°–179° IN ONE FRAME**. Two causes: a half turn's way round was the rounding's (`rollUp`'s, and `screenUpTarget`'s blend between
   the face's up and gravity's, opposite just past straight above); and the roll was started only before the camera moved in the frame. ✅ The way
   round latched (`rollSense`, `signedTurn`), the blend's side the one nearer the up before, the roll started where the camera's up is computed.
2. **"Clamp the angular travel per mm input for any face"** — ✅ **THE VIEW'S TURN GOVERNED** (`viewTurnGoverned`): the camera's look and its roll
   together (`frameTurn`) at most `orbitViewTurnCap` × the dx yaw's own °/mm (1 by default: the vertical face's pace) — a yaw on a vertical face is
   never cut; straight above a top a dx spinning the picture, a dy tipping it over the edge are. ⭐ **A roll takes the finger's travel FIRST, at that
   cap** (`rollTakes`): the piece waits while the camera rolls — half a turn ~35 mm, linear (⛔ eased, mid-roll it turned 1.5× the cap). Measured: the
   roll over the top 4.9°/mm, the orbit 5°/mm; no frame over 10°.
3. **"Limitation of delta position input to the gizmo"** — ✅ the shown gizmo's step held to `gizmoMmPerMm` (40) × the finger's travel that frame +
   a 0.5 m/s walk at rest (`gizmoStepCap`). Measured: the largest step 50–70 mm a frame (⛔ 347–525 mm: over the thin top, under the bottom).
4. **"The green or red sections are strange"** (attachments 1, 2) — the gizmo's vertex was always joined to its nearer neighbour, at any length: a
   held drop (from under the floor) or a step in depth drew a long stray segment. ✅ Forced only within twice a join's reach (`gizmoJoins`'
   `maxForced`). ⚠ Not reproduced exactly headless; a device look should confirm.
