## ⭐⭐⭐ THE ONE THAT MATTERED: `D124` was too wide, and siblings were still grabbed

`D124` (*a second press on another body steers the held one; it never grabs it*) exempted **the held
body's whole ASSEMBLY**, to keep the unsnap's second touch — which lands on the seated Follower —
a hold. ⛔ But two parts both seated on the frozen plate share an assembly, so **holding one and pressing
the other still grabbed it**, and a tap there aligned and unseated it: the exact thing `D124` forbids.
⭐ Found by reading which conditions still reached the press-time alignment path.
✅ The exemption is now **the held body's seated partner** (`isSeatedCouple`), and a vector names the
sibling case (red against the old rule). ⭐ `METHOD`: *an exception scoped by a container ("the same
assembly") is wider than the case that asked for it ("the unsnap's partner") — state the case.*
