## Why

The owner, verbatim: *"I find it extremely difficult to control the gizmo: the red and green sections have strange contours, sometimes the contours stop
somewhere in the face, the gizmo do not follow the delta position input, the gizmo jumps, the protruding parts further complicate the red and green
sections, the scene gets slanted vs. the face and i cannot see very well where is the gizmo. I need better implementation of the orbit tilt. I think once
the orbit is tilted to follow a face orientation, the red and green sections do not need to fully circumference the assembly, but they only need to span
the hosting coplanar faces: this way, this will avoid intricate green and red contours and jumps of gizmo over singularities such as edge, etc."*

Each complaint had one cause in the carrier (`section_hull.ts`): a point on the 2-D hulls of slices through the WHOLE assembly, dropped by raycast — a drop
that changes surface jumps (the 18 cm onto `Piece44`), a missed or deep drop breaks a line, the pace is the loop's (not the finger's), the face frame and
the lock cone slant the view, ~100 picks a moving frame. The slide already did what the owner asked, one level lower (a plane, a coplanar region, a clamp
that slides) — the face-span extends that up to the engage distance.

## The owner's decisions (verbatim, then as built)

1. *"Leaving a face: Option B. Design the tilt and catch properly so the movements of the piece and camera are blended properly."* → a push past the
   region's edge goes into a **CATCH** (`faceSpanCatchMm`, 3 mm of finger), then the **WRAP** onto the face across — convex (a box's next side) or
   concave (an inner corner's wall) — the face's normal, the piece's direction, the camera and the screen's up turned about the edge by the finger's
   travel along the push (`faceSpanTurnMm`, 18 mm — the orbit's own 5.1°/mm for 90°), **reversible** (back the other way: share → 0, onto the first face
   again, the edge point kept). Nothing across (the floor, a slot too far): the edge holds, named on the HUD.
2. *"Face-span engages: make a slider. I prefer to engage as far as reasonably possible so that the orbit mechanism is the key for the user to position the
   piece on the assembly early"* → `faceSpanEngageM` **4.5 m** (CAMERA ORBIT AROUND CENTER), out past **1.2 ×** (5.4 m). ⭐ Why 4.5: the far orbit sits
   at ring 1's distance, **6.0 m** on `Scene_1` (the approach coordinate 1); the release must stay reachable below it, so the engage is held at most
   far / 1.22 = 4.9 m (`spanEngageM`); 4.5 m leaves a margin of one approach step. The boot is far out (6 m): the far orbit picks the face, any approach
   in (~4 mm of A + drag / two fingers) engages.
3. *"constant gizmo speed, which shall follow the current orbit angular speed on dy and dx"* → the gizmo's step = the ORBIT's own step this frame (the
   finger's, with every gain, and the coast) × the assembly's radius (`spanStepOf`): 0.283 m × 5.1°/mm ≈ **25 mm of gizmo per mm of finger** on
   `Scene_1`, the same on every face.
4. *"Direction: … the behavior of the gizmo in commit 65a691aef4171f9ed64f03242e7486c38eb72384 was OK (gizmo following the orbit). I want to keep this
   behavior."* → the axes are latched at the engage from where the orbit's yaw and pitch steps moved the piece (`spanAxes` of `frameSteps` in the orbit's
   frame; `asmSign` untouched), so the same finger moves the gizmo on the face the way the orbit moved it; on a wall they are levelled (dx horizontal,
   dy the wall's up — senses kept); at rest after a roll they are read off the screen by the orbit's own rule (`axisOnScreen` ≡ `signOnScreen`); at the
   release the finger's signs are carried back onto the orbit (`signAfter`).
5. *"Gizmo shall pass over the protruding part. Blend the movements to avoid jumps, etc."* → protrusions are NOT holes: the gizmo rides the FRONTMOST
   surface of the parts standing on the plane (`coveredTop` — their triangles, barycentric, no ray pick), eased by the finger's travel
   (`faceSpanRideMm`, 3 mm) and by time at rest; the piece keeps its shell distance; the lines follow the tops, a step drawn as a near-vertical connector
   (`spanPolyline`). ⛔ A part must TOUCH the plane to be ridden (`SPAN_TOUCH_M` 2.5 mm — a seated part stands one contour margin, 1.5 mm, off): across an
   empty 16 mm slot a neighbour read as a protrusion and the gizmo leapt onto its far side (found headless, this build).
6. *"Roll freeze: I liked what was implemented in 65a691a. I am OK to have the 180 roll, provided this is not too often due to other logic flaws or bad
   implementation this build will strive to eliminate."* → the roll goes on WITH the orbit over `orbitTiltBlendMm` (`rollShare`, as at `d8134eb`); ⛔
   `rollTakes` (the piece waiting while the camera rolled — the "freeze") unwired, kept as declared debt; kept: the latched sense (`rollSense`,
   `signedTurn`), the up-blend side nearer the previous up, the roll started in `assemblyCameraFrame`. ⚠ `65a691a` itself is the revert to `ff2fca2`, which
   had no roll at all; the roll the owner describes is `d8134eb`'s (the commit after it) — read so, to be confirmed. `viewTurnGoverned` is kept for the
   far orbit only (it cuts a step, it never waits) and returns 1 in the face-span (its step turns no view).

## Open — for the owner, on the device

1. The gizmo's speed: ~25 mm per mm of finger (the orbit's pace × 0.283 m) — fast for fine placement; the orbit's gains are its only control (decision 3).
2. The 180° roll: on the back face reached over the top or the bottom (the up carried round is upside down; a wall's up is the world's) — rolled over
   10 mm of travel while the gizmo goes on. Is it too often?
3. The engage at 4.5 m: the far orbit only between 5.4 and 6 m (the boot). Any approach in engages.
4. Unplaced pieces leave HOLES: the painting's top and bottom are split where Piece1, Piece2, Piece17, Piece23, Piece41 are missing; a push into a slot
   wraps onto its inner side faces (convex edges) — physically true, possibly surprising.
5. The ride: 3 mm of finger for a step (≈ 75 mm of gizmo) — the ring floats in front of the face for that long after leaving a protrusion.
6. ⛔ Not built: a wrap between PARALLEL planes (a step in the face — the gizmo stops at it); the far orbit keeps its carrier and sections (only far out
   now); a device look (rule 5).
