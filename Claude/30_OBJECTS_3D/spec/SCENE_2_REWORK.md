## 1. Why — in plain words

`Scene_2` (the glass V6 on its plinth: `Assets/Blender/World0_Scene2_engine/`, built by `Assets/Blender/scripts/build_engine.py`, exported to
`world0_scene_2_engine.glb`) was drawn on 2026-11-12 – 13, BEFORE the orbit on charts and the surface's rules existed. ⛔ It has never been played
with the charts, and P2 (the landing that reads the piece's whole body) drives on `Scene_1` only (`READOUT_SCENE`, `src/render/landing_readout.ts`). The checks built by the surface work read its exported file and find it breaks the drawing rules almost
everywhere — the gizmo's skin off the parts, open, deep hollows unlidded, collision pieces apart from what is drawn, parts with nothing to rest on.
`GM13` redraws the parts by the rules, settles the decisions tied to the scene, and makes `Scene_2` play with today's orbit.

## 3. The decisions that come with it — ⏳ HIS WORD, asked at the start of `GM13` (a study first: his four-part method)

1. **The frozen plinth (ledger rows 6, 21).** The plinth E01 is FROZEN (pre-placed), and the game skips a frozen part twice: the face map
   (`placedFaces`) — so its top, 2 719 cm², the scene's largest face, is NO PLACE for the gizmo at any stage — and the orbited piece's collision
   (read in the code, not yet seen in the game). Options: (a) the game reads a frozen part other than the FLOOR (the floor stays out: it is not a
   part — his rule); (b) the plinth stops being frozen (pre-placed, goal-locked like a seated part). ⛔ Check first in the pick-up flow (row 6).
2. **The ten ways in that start at no place (row 22, S8's B4).** E02 (onto the frozen plinth — decision 1 settles it); E06 (once E05 stands, the +z
   bank facet shrinks to a sliver — its seat 55 mm outside it: the ORDER decides — E06 before E05, or the rule changes); the six pistons and the two
   heads (their bank's facet ends 4.6 mm short of their seats: the TOP facet takes the bores' mouths — their plunge starts from a facet 30° off their
   axis). Construction rule **C5** (`SCENE_WORKFLOW.md` §10.3, ⏳ PROPOSED) says what a scene must satisfy; his word on C5 with it.
3. **The plunge** (`ORBIT_SURFACE.md` §2.5 — S13 + S28: a goal with a narrow entry set, the ghost at the mouth, then a guarded move down the axis) —
   the pistons down their bores. Left out of the surface work on purpose (`ORBIT_SURFACE.md` §5.4); B1 against the drawn meshes runs first (row 3).
4. **Sealed and buried faces** (`ORBIT_SURFACE_L1.md` §8 Q1): no real case before the redraw (E17's inward half is a drawing defect, row 14); built
   with the redrawn parts only if one appears.
5. **P2 driving on `Scene_2`**: `READOUT_SCENE` is `Scene_1` alone today; on `Scene_2` the cast's cost per stage is measured (`check_landing_cost.ts`,
   S14: a turn frame 1 160 ms → 0.19 ms with the cast) — extend it behind its switch, gated on his records.
6. **The PROPOSED construction rules** of `SCENE_WORKFLOW.md` §10.3 (C1 – C5) and the playability brief (§7.1): B4's *"no direction starts at a
   place"* becomes a FAIL only with the brief — his word.

## 5. The rules that bind `GM13`

* Docs before build; root cause before patch; one owner per thing — no smoothing or undo layer over the logic; a failed build: question the spec
  first.
* Every new vector shown RED on a mutant first; `npm run verify` alone before a code commit; the doc gates (`tests/doc_links`, `tests/doc_budget`)
  before a docs commit; commit by explicit path, never `git add -A`.
* ⛔ The closed orbit is not reopened: a change that would move `Scene_1`'s camera or gizmo goes behind a switch, 0 = LIVE, gated bit for bit.
* ⛔ Heavy jobs one at a time (Blender builds, the one command ≈ 6 min, headless replays); never while he tests on this PC.
* ⛔ The floor is not a part; never a 3D orientation widget; patent-free methods; the solver's budget 24 queries a frame.
* Every lesson becomes a rule of construction or drawing written for a script (`SCENE_WORKFLOW.md` §10), then a check, then a ledger row.
